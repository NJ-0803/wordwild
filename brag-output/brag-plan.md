# Brag Plan: Wordwild

## What is this app?
Wordwild turns every English word you meet into a word you remember: save it in seconds, understand it in simple words (with Hindi help), review it just before you forget, and watch a Township-style city grow from every word you log.

## The angle
A quiet, confident product film. The product IS the proof: a real first word saved in seconds, a real review, a real puzzle, and a city that keeps growing. Signature "NJ" opens it, the way a maker signs their work.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An italic "NJ" signature writes itself across a dark navy glass panel. "Learn the words you meet." settles beneath it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Type "serendipity" into the one-box first screen, press Enter, and the meaning appears with the Hindi line, then "I know it" earns +3 points.
- A hard Unscramble puzzle solved by voice: "resilient" lights up, the whole card shakes like a small earthquake, the timer stops at 0:13, friends' times appear.
- The city: a two-house hamlet becomes a town, a small city, then a planned city of avenues, roundabouts, farms and parks, with the word count climbing.

## Outro / punchline
"Every word you log builds your city." then the Wordwild wordmark, wordwild-seven.vercel.app and github.com/NJ-0803/wordwild.

## User flow worth showing
Entry (one box) -> key action (save + know it) -> result (a city that grows).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film, dark navy glass, Apple-like restraint
- Interpretation: four scenes, long readable holds, slow reveals landing on the music's strong beats, no jokes.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #060a16 (navy black) with #6e82ff and teal glows
- Accent: #6e82ff (primary), #65b96e (progress)
- Text: #f3f5fb
- Display font: Instrument Serif (italic for the signature)
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the real Township-style city, and the glass first-word box

## Share copy (draft)
I built Wordwild: save a word in seconds, remember it, and watch a city grow from every word you learn. wordwild-seven.vercel.app

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse, motion-matched accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9 (114.8 BPM), trimmed to 18s, fade-in 0.5s, fade-out from 16.4s
- Music treatment: sits under everything (volume ~0.5), fades out under the end card
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Strong cues 3.70, 6.34, 8.44, 10.54, 12.65. Locks: meaning reveal 6.34s, puzzle quake 10.54s, city reveal 12.65s. Scene cuts on 3.70, 8.44, 12.65.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass breathes the background glows, treble lifts the glass edge light. No visualizer graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched: keypresses while typing, a click on Enter, a soft select on the reveal, a switch on "I know it", a low impact on the quake, one bong at the end.
- Restraint rule: nothing loud; SFX never cover the music's strong beats.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.7s
NJ signature writes in (0.3-1.6s), flourish draws, "Learn the words you meet." settles from 2.0s and holds to 3.4s.
Sequential/interaction: signature wipe, then underline draw.
Audio intent: calm, confident opening. Audio-coupled idea: none.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Save and know it — 4.7s
Glass window rises at 3.7s: "Save your first word", the word typed letter by letter (4.3-5.6s), Enter at 5.8s; at 6.34s (beat lock) the meaning card replaces it: serendipity, the meaning, the Hindi line; review buttons appear at 7.3s and "I know it" is pressed at 7.9s with a "+3 points" chip.
Sequential/interaction: yes — typing, Enter, reveal, button press.
Audio intent: tactile and satisfying. Audio-coupled idea: keypress per letter, click on Enter, select on reveal, switch on "I know it".
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Play — 4.2s
Unscramble (Hard): clue card, the mic pill, and at 10.54s (beat lock) the letters of "resilient" pop into place one after another, the card quakes, a ring expands, the timer reads 0:13, then a friends board slides in.
Sequential/interaction: yes — letters pop in sequence; friends rows.
Audio intent: a small payoff. Audio-coupled idea: low impact on the quake.
Transition mood: hard, on the beat → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The city — 5.35s
Full-bleed real city plates crossfade as the word count climbs: 6 → 25 → 150 → 400 words, Hamlet → Town → Small city → Big city, slow push-in. "Every word you log builds your city." Then the end card: Wordwild, the URLs, and NJ.
Sequential/interaction: yes — four plates and a counter.
Audio intent: lift, then land softly. Audio-coupled idea: switch at the wipe; bong on the end card.
Transition mood: soft → end

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, warm, understated
**Audio summary:** a warm bed, small tactile clicks while typing, one satisfying reveal on beat, one low hit on the quake, a soft bell on the end card.
