# Wordwild town economy and levels (2026-09-26)

Goal: the game should make a learner want to learn real words, never to grind or fear losing something.

## Two separate currencies
- **XP -> level.** Earned from learning. Never spent, never lost, so a level can only go up.
  Level steps: 50, 60, 70, 80... XP to reach the next level. Early levels come fast; later ones feel earned.
- **Coins.** Earned from the same events, but spent on buildings. Spending never lowers a level.

## Where points come from (all from real learning events)
| Event | Points | Guard |
|---|---|---|
| Save a new word (with a known meaning) | 5 | 40 a day (8 words), once per word |
| Advance a review stage (independent, on time) | 10 | daily mastery cap 100 |
| Word becomes secure | 30 | once per word; counts toward the cap |
| Finish a story scene | 12 coins, 12 XP | once per scene, replay free |
| Daily order collected | per order | 3 a day, unclaimed ones vanish silently |
| Welcome back after 3+ days away | 15 | once per returning day |

One fully mastered word is about 55 points. The last building needs roughly 8-9 mastered words of XP.

## What levels do
- Level unlocks buildings (chapters) and their story scenes; coins pay for building. Both must be met.
- Pace target: a first building in about 1-2 sessions, the whole town in a few weeks of light use.

## What is deliberately not here
- No streaks, no loss when you miss days, no wilting crops (the welcome-back gift pays you for returning).
- No timers that force you to return, no barn limits, no premium gems, no leaderboards.
- No coins for lookups, page views or time spent. Only for saved words (capped), stages reached, scenes and orders.

## Why it resists farming
Saving words pays only a few a day, mastery has a daily cap, and each reward key is paid once per word or scene. Stage rewards need an independent correct answer at the right time, so tapping through does not pay.

## Ideas not built yet
Weekly event (bigger shared goal), chapter collections/badges when all words of a chapter are secure, gifts to other learners. Balance numbers are a first guess and should be tuned with real learners.
